CHAPTERS 3–4 · ROBBERIES · YASMANI CABRERA

GTA 6: all of Yasmani's jobs — robbery order, safe codes and the Chapter 4 trap

Updated November 28, 2026. Yasmani Cabrera is a fence for jewellery and marked bills in Little Cuba. His jobs stretch across two chapters: three jewellery stores first, then ATMs and payday loans, followed by the Gold Blammer, small-bank tips and an armoured security van. This guide lays out the practical order, exact codes and a warning about a mission that can cause some of his optional tips to be skipped.

Important: this is the practical order reported by current game guides, not an official Rockstar mission chart. Some stages are separate purchasable tips and can be skipped, so do not treat the list as one mandatory linear story chain.

1. Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs: all three safe codes

After Boobie Time, Yasmani contacts Jason and Lucia and asks them to retrieve one specific piece from each of three jewellery stores. The stores can be hit in any order, but the most useful route starts with the smallest shop: after your first sale, Yasmani gives you the Snap Gun, which opens certain rear doors without the usual forced entry and immediate alarm.

  1. Start with Casa de Empeño: it is small and has no rear door that requires the Snap Gun.
  2. Open the safe with 1680, take the Buffalo Skull Chain and return to Yasmani.
  3. Sell the first item in person. Collect the Snap Gun from the shop when the game offers it as your reward.
  4. At Richardson Jewelry, use the Snap Gun on the rear door, find the safe and enter 6815.
  5. At Donovan Banks & Co., use the Snap Gun at the back and enter 0104. Keep the leading zero.
  6. Before leaving, use the available Destroy Footage interaction at the CCTV box. At the last two stores you can leave through the rear door; the front display cases are extra loot but raise the risk of an alarm.
  7. Sell all three requested pieces to Yasmani. Guides put the target-item payout at about $20,000 total; loose cash and other loot are separate.

Why do Casa de Empeño first? Selling its item unlocks the Snap Gun, which makes the rear entrances at Richardson and Donovan easier to use quietly. Do not waste time hunting for other codes: these three combinations appear consistently across several walkthroughs.

2. Yasmani's ATM Jobs: three loaded ATMs

After completing Jewelry Jobs and the story mission Credit Check, Yasmani offers his first ATM tip for free. It marks three ATMs that are temporarily loaded with cash:

  • Megamart ATM
  • Casino ATM
  • Xero Gas ATM

The window is short: several guides report that these three marked ATMs stay full for only one in-game day. If you miss the window, you lose the cash from those three locations, but later paid tips from Yasmani remain available.

  1. Get a vehicle with a winch. The easiest option is the 1986 Declasse Rancher parked behind Yasmani's shop in Little Cuba.
  2. At Megamart and Xero Gas, reverse up to the ATM, attach the winch, pull it out of the wall and drag it until the casing breaks.
  3. Smash the four cash trays that fall out and collect the marked bills.
  4. For the Casino ATM, guides describe a quicker alternative: attach a Sticky Bomb to the machine and detonate it instead of towing it.
  5. Return the marked bills to Yasmani. Guides estimate roughly $30,000–$40,000 for all three locations, before the exact fence calculation in your save.

Use the Dye Pack Jammer from Credit Check when handling marked bills. Do not confuse the map's loot value with clean cash: marked money must be sold to a fence.

3. Payday Loan Jobs: two locations from a paid tip

After Jewelry Jobs and Credit Check, you can buy Yasmani's next tip for about $1,000. It marks two payday-loan stores holding extra cash that day. The exact pair can vary between playthroughs.

  1. Buy the tip from Yasmani and open both map markers.
  2. Carry Sticky Bombs in case a keypad door blocks the way. A plain rear door may open with the Snap Gun without an immediate alarm; guides say a keypad door must be blown open and sets the alarm off.
  3. Inside, aim at the teller and take the cash placed on the counter.
  4. Do not leave yet: go to the back room and open the safe. Both the counter cash and the safe contents are needed for the robbery to count as complete.
  5. Lose the wanted level, repeat at the second store and sell the marked bills to Yasmani.

Published walkthroughs use roughly $12,000 from one safe as a guide figure, but the actual amount depends on the location, loot, tip cost and fence cut. If a marker remains unfinished, check whether you left either the counter or the safe untouched.

4. Steal the Gold Blammer: do not leave it until late Chapter 4

After at least one Payday Loan tip/job, ask Yasmani for another lead. He mentions a gold necklace called the Gold Blammer, hidden in one of four Rockridge stores. Several guides point to Vice City Gun & Pawn: the necklace is in a locked case on a desk in the back room. The store has armed security. Take the necklace back to Yasmani; the reported reward is $15,000.

Source caveat: Yasmani's tip does not name the exact shop, so if the item is not in Vice City Gun & Pawn in your save, check the other three marked locations. Guides agree on four possible stores and the $15,000 reward, but not every walkthrough describes the search order the same way.

5. Yasmani's Bank Jobs: each tip supplies its own safe code

In Chapter 4, Yasmani sells tips for small-bank robberies. Each purchased tip marks a bank and sends you the safe code. Do not reuse one code at another branch: the combination belongs to that specific robbery.

  1. Buy a tip and travel to the marked bank.
  2. If the door into the teller area supports the Snap Gun, open it quietly.
  3. A keypad door to the back rooms may require a Sticky Bomb and trigger the alarm.
  4. Open the safe with the code from Yasmani's message, take the marked bills and check whether you can disable the alarm and destroy camera footage before leaving.
  5. Sell the loot to Yasmani. Guides use about $20,000 per safe as a rough figure, not a guaranteed payout.

6. The ordering trap: collect the extra tips before That's Where The Money Is…

This is the most important warning in this guide. The VI Field Guide, updated November 28, and separate walkthroughs report that if you complete the Chapter 4 optional mission That's Where The Money Is… first, Yasmani's strand may jump straight to its final job and skip Steal the Gold Blammer and the Bank Jobs tip. These jobs provide extra cash and optional content, even though sources do not list them as separate requirements for trophies or 100% completion.

Practical order: after Payday Loan Jobs, ask Yasmani for the next tip and finish Gold Blammer. In Chapter 4, collect any available Bank Jobs before starting That's Where The Money Is… . If you want to see all of his jobs, do not leave your conversations with Yasmani until the end of the chapter.

7. Tail the Security Van: the final $10,000 tip

After at least two Payday Loan robberies and the Chapter 4 mission That's Where The Money Is…, Yasmani offers a security-van tip for about $10,000. Follow the armoured van to a bank and enter the vault while the guard opens it.

  1. Buy the tip and head to the marked van.
  2. Keep your distance. If the driver notices you tailing him, he may not stop at the bank.
  3. When the van parks, wait for the guard to enter with the case, then follow him toward the vault.
  4. Take the marked bills from the cart. If there are more than two bags, hand one to your partner or switch characters to keep loading.
  5. Check the back room safe too, if you have time and a clear route.
  6. Expect a four-star wanted level after the robbery. Plan your escape before entering, lose the police and sell the marked bills to Yasmani.

The payout depends on the bank and how much you carry out. Do not plan your getaway as if four stars will disappear as soon as you leave: guides warn that police may already be waiting outside.

What is confirmed, and what still needs a caveat

  • Consistent across multiple guides: the three jewellery safe codes are 1680, 6815 and 0104; the Snap Gun follows the first sale; the first ATM tip is free; the three ATMs have a one-day window; and Tail the Security Van requires a careful tail and ends with four wanted stars.
  • Current sequence: The VI Field Guide updated its Yasmani coverage on November 28 and specifically flagged the risk of losing the Gold Blammer and Bank Jobs tips after That's Where The Money Is… .
  • Not Rockstar-confirmed: exact payouts, ATM locations and skip conditions come from game databases and walkthroughs, not an official Rockstar step-by-step guide.
  • Trophies: PowerPyx marks Yasmani among the contacts that do not count toward the silver Strangers & Freaks trophy. Do not confuse that with separate robbery tasks or the progress shown in the in-game menus.
