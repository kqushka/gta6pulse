SECRET SIDE MISSION · NO FLUSHERS · STAMINA

GTA 6 The Final Flush: How to Find the QR Code and Get a Permanent Stamina Boost

Updated November 27, 2026. The hidden No Flushers chain does not begin with a normal mission marker. Find a “Stop! Don't Flush” poster in a public restroom, scan its QR code with WAiNK, and follow the messages to Dr. Merle. The bizarre trip to a sewage treatment plant ends with a permanent increase to maximum stamina — but only if you enter the chamber yourself after flushing Merle.

Quick answer

Find the No Flushers poster in the men's restroom on Corsair Key, scan it with WAiNK and reply TRUTH to the automated text. Keep the code 00-00-02. Visit Frank's Waffles in Tequesta at night, talk to Dr. Merle and give him the code. Follow him to the treatment plant, solve the three-light control panel and enter the chamber yourself after sending Merle through.

Do not skip the final action: published walkthroughs say the maximum-stamina boost appears after your character enters the chamber. Pressing the button for Merle alone is not enough.

1. Find the “Stop! Don't Flush” poster

  1. Travel to Corsair Key, a small island northeast of Key Lento in the Leonida Keys.
  2. Look for the public restroom block near the north end of the island. Enter the men's restroom and find the No Flushers poster on the wall or stall area. It reads “Stop! Don't Flush” and includes a QR code.
  3. Open the WAiNK app on your in-game phone and scan the code.
  4. Wait for a text from Automated Machine and reply TRUTH. Read the next message carefully: it contains the code 00-00-02 for your conversation with the contact.
  5. If the game presents another confirmation prompt, follow the phone's instruction. One updated guide documents an additional OKAY reply after TRUTH; other walkthroughs move straight to the follow-up message.

Other reported poster locations: guides also list the Watson Bay campground restroom and a stall at Nine1Nine. Hold To Reset additionally mentions the gym near the Chapter 3 apartment and a Port Gellhorn notice board. Lists differ, so Corsair Key is the clearest starting landmark, not necessarily the only poster.

2. Meet Dr. Merle at Frank's Waffles

  1. After replying to the message, check the map for Frank's Waffles in Tequesta, Vice City.
  2. Go there at night. GamesRadar+ and GTABase report that the marker may be locked during the day and Dr. Merle may not be inside. If the meeting is still unavailable, try another time or advance one story mission; Hold To Reset gives that additional workaround.
  3. Look for the man sitting in a booth near the restrooms. When he asks for the code, focus on him, open the dialogue choice and give 00-00-02.
  4. After the conversation, follow Merle outside and get behind the wheel of his van.

3. Drive the van to the treatment plant

Dr. Merle gives spoken directions during the drive. PowerPyx notes that this leg may not show a normal HUD objective: start by heading south, turn east at the intersection he specifies, then continue along the highway. Listen to your passenger rather than ignoring his directions.

Sources differ on the regional label for the facility: PowerPyx and GTABase call it Gloriana Key, while Dexerto uses Hamlet. Follow Dr. Merle's directions and the mission marker rather than relying only on a third-party region label.

4. Help Merle enter the facility

  1. Park where instructed and follow Merle to the fence around the treatment plant.
  2. While he works on the lock, watch the road. Warn him when a worker's vehicle approaches so he can hide.
  3. If the driver notices you and asks what you are doing, choose the calm Defuse response, as recommended by PowerPyx.
  4. Once the gate opens, head to the highlighted building. Move carefully and use the side entrance.
  5. If workers inside ask who you are, Dexerto recommends choosing Lie and saying you are electricians.

5. Solve the control-panel puzzle

  1. Inside, Merle cannot find a way down to the tracker. Climb the metal stairs to the upper walkway.
  2. Find the console with three indicator lights on the left and a large lever on the right.
  3. Press the buttons beside the two red indicators — the middle and bottom lights. Your goal is to make all three lights green.
  4. Then tilt or activate the large lever on the right. A hidden staircase opens in the floor.
  5. Go downstairs and follow Merle to the chamber.

If the stairs do not open, return to the console and make sure all three lights are green. The guides agree on the same solution: the two red buttons on the left, then the lever on the right.

6. Claim the permanent stamina boost

  1. When Dr. Merle climbs into the large chamber, press the red button on the control panel in front of it to flush him through the system.
  2. After the loudspeaker warning, do not leave the room.
  3. Enter the chamber yourself. GamesRadar+, Dexerto and Hold To Reset say this triggers the final sequence and returns your character home with a message that maximum stamina has increased.

PowerPyx lists The Final Flush in the Strangers & Freaks chain and says it counts toward the related trophy/achievement. Do not confuse that with a separate 100% completion requirement: check your in-game Progress screen rather than assuming every optional mission is automatically mandatory for 100%.

Which protagonist should you choose?

Guides do not describe character participation in exactly the same way. PowerPyx says the mission can be started as Lucia or Jason solo; Hold To Reset calls it a solo mission that can be completed by either character, but not both; GTABase says the permanent boost goes to the protagonist who performs the mission.

Practical advice: choose the protagonist whose maximum stamina you most want to improve before starting. Do not assume the reward is shared between both characters or that you can repeat the mission with the other one.

What is confirmed — and what needs a caveat

  • Consistent across several guides: the No Flushers QR poster, TRUTH reply, Dr. Merle meeting, code 00-00-02, the three-green-light panel and a permanent maximum-stamina increase.
  • Supported by multiple walkthroughs: the men's restroom on Corsair Key is a reliable starting point; visit Frank's Waffles at night; enter the chamber yourself after flushing Merle.
  • Disputed details: the full list of alternate poster locations, the region name for the treatment plant and whether an extra confirmation reply appears after TRUTH.
  • Character and reward: guides describe the mission as a solo choice between Jason and Lucia; select the protagonist carefully because the reward is not described as shared.
  • Editorial limitation: Rockstar has not published an official walkthrough for this secret mission. GTA6Pulse compared independent gameplay guides and does not claim to have personally completed it.
