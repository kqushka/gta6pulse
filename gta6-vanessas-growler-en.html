GTA 6: How to Get Vanessa's Growler and the Del Rio Boulevard Garage

Updated November 26, 2026. In Chapter 3, you can unlock the laptop in the Bayside apartment, read a message from Unknown and receive the code for a garage in La Perle. Behind the shutter is a pink Pfister Growler, and the property becomes the Del Rio Boulevard Garage. This guide follows the clue trail from the apartment to the keypad.

Quick answer

Laptop answers: Muffins → Queen → 15. After logging in, open WhatUp and read the chat from Unknown. Garage code: 92665. Enter it at the keypad beside the grey shutter near Auntie's Place in La Perle.

When to do it: after Welcome to Vice City, once the Bayside Apartments are available. Several published guides recommend claiming the car and garage before Chapter 3 ends, because access to the apartment may disappear after the chapter transition. This warning comes from walkthrough reporting, not an official Rockstar notice.

1. Unlock the laptop in the Bayside apartment

  1. Complete Welcome to Vice City and enter the apartment in Bayside Apartments.
  2. Find the laptop in the bedroom, near the bed.
  3. Inspect the clues around the apartment. The framed dog photo gives the name Muffins; the drawing of a dog wearing a crown points to Queen; the note on the back of the drawing refers to the class of '15.
  4. Return to the laptop and choose the answers in this order: Muffins, then Queen, then 15. These are three separate choices, not one phrase to type as a single password.

2. Get the code from WhatUp

  1. After logging in, open the WhatUp app on the laptop.
  2. Select the conversation with the contact named Unknown.
  3. Read the message: it points Vanessa to a gift in La Perle, next to Auntie's Place, and gives the five-digit code 92665.
  4. Make sure the message has been read and the code has been added to the game's notes or prompts. If the garage keypad does not respond, return to the laptop and check the Unknown chat rather than relying on having logged in alone.

Do not rely on entering 92665 before completing the laptop sequence. Several guides report that the keypad interaction becomes available only after reading the message.

3. Find Auntie's Place in La Perle

Auntie's Place is a small takeaway restaurant, not a story mission. It may not appear as a normal selectable business on the map. Use two landmarks to find the block: Twa Ponya Tattoo and Aline's Thrift Store. The location is in La Perle, around Del Rio Boulevard.

  1. Travel to La Perle and locate Twa Ponya Tattoo.
  2. Check the opposite side of the road and the parking area behind the row of shops; Aline's Thrift Store helps identify the right block.
  3. Look for the small takeaway spot with the Auntie's Place sign.
  4. Do not search inside the restaurant for the car. The garage is behind it and slightly to the right, in the yard between the buildings.

Do not mix them up: Auntie's Place and Aunt Zola's Soul Food are different businesses. Use Twa Ponya Tattoo and Aline's Thrift Store as your main landmarks.

4. Open the garage with code 92665

  1. Approach the small pale concrete garage with a grey roller shutter.
  2. Find the keypad to the right of the shutter. A pink bow is painted above it; a parking warning sign may be nearby.
  3. Interact with the keypad and enter 92665.
  4. Wait for the shutter to rise. Inside is the pink Vanessa's Growler, a variant of the Pfister Growler.

After opening it, the location is named Del Rio Boulevard Garage and provides storage for one vehicle. Published guides also report a weapon locker inside.

What you get

  • Vanessa's Growler: a pink Pfister sports car parked inside the garage.
  • Del Rio Boulevard Garage: an additional one-car storage location.
  • A new La Perle location: the garage remains a useful landmark after it is unlocked.

Registration caveat. Sources disagree: GamesRadar and GTA EXP say the car is automatically added to the player's vehicles; GAMES.GG and GTA6.house distinguish opening the garage from registering a personal vehicle. Check the Growler's status in your own vehicle menu after claiming it. If the game offers registration at Pay 'n' Spray, use the price and status shown in your save. We do not promise free registration or quote an unverified sale price.

What is confirmed — and what needs a caveat

  • Consistent across several independent guides: the Muffins → Queen → 15 sequence, the Unknown chat, code 92665, La Perle and the pink Pfister Growler.
  • Supported by multiple routes: the garage is beside Auntie's Place; Twa Ponya Tattoo and Aline's Thrift Store are useful landmarks.
  • Missable-content warning: published guides recommend claiming the reward in Chapter 3 while the apartment is accessible. Rockstar has not published an exact final deadline.
  • Unsettled: whether the Growler is automatically registered as a personal vehicle and the conditions for selling it. Check its status in-game before leaving.
  • Editorial limitation: Rockstar has not published this combination as an official walkthrough. GTA6Pulse compared published gameplay guides and does not claim to have personally completed this route.
