What to do before Tourist Trap: a Chapter 1 checklist

Tourist Trap starts a linked Chapter 1 finale. After it begins, free roaming with both characters is restricted for several hours until Chapter 2 opens up. This checklist helps you finish the content you want before that transition.

First: make a separate save

Conservative deadline: finish this list before Recalibration if it is available, and save to a separate slot. Some guides report that Recalibration flows directly into Tourist Trap. Sources differ on the exact trigger sequence, so a backup save is the safest approach.

Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home and Discharge Time run as a linked sequence. VGC reports that free roaming as both Jason and Lucia is restricted for several hours during Chapter 2; you can return to Key Lento later.

Checklist

Confirmed details versus cautious advice

  • Cross-checked by multiple guides: Tourist Trap starts the linked Chapter 1 finale; Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter disappear after it starts.
  • Reported guide advice: losses of cash/stash at the Chapter 1 transition. Saving separately and securing money is prudent, but we do not present the loss details as official confirmation.
  • Trophy precaution: Lucia's balcony meditation is missable, but guides are not certain whether it is required for Positive Mental Attitude; it is not required for 100% completion.
  • House repairs: finish them before Lucia returns, as guides describe the opportunity as missable.
