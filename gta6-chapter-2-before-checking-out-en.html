GTA 6 Chapter 2: what to do before Checking Out

Updated November 25, 2026. Near the end of Chapter 2, the game asks you to save at least $15,000 and finish the other main story jobs in the chapter. This unlocks Checking Out, the move to Vice City. Starting that mission closes Starlet Motel as your home, so check the motel pickups and keep a separate manual save before you leave.

Quick checklist

  1. Open Map → Story and check the remaining Chapter 2 main jobs.
  2. Reach $15,000. The clearest confirmed payout at this stage is Vicki's $10,000 after Afternoon Delight; it arrives through BuckMe on the next in-game day.
  3. If you are short, sell a tracker-free car to Moses, check available robberies, and collect mission payouts.
  4. Before moving, pick up the Vicki and Victor Maravilla cards at Starlet Motel. Also check the Bryson Powers card and the two magazines; sources are less consistent about their post-chapter status.
  5. Make a manual save in a separate slot before starting Checking Out.
  6. Once the money and story list are complete, wait for Cal's text and the Move to Vice City objective. Do not start the finale until you have checked the motel.

Important: Checking Out is the chapter transition. Port Gellhorn itself remains accessible afterwards; what you lose is Starlet Motel as a home with its room and safehouse functions.

1. Which jobs should you finish?

After Coffee Break, check Map → Story instead of relying on a universal mission number from another walkthrough. Some Chapter 2 jobs can be tackled in different orders, and guide databases do not always count story and side activities the same way.

  • Hit Avery Pawn — rob the pawn shop.
  • Hit Kingpin Bowling — rob the bowling alley.
  • Impounded Vehicle — recover the impounded car.
  • Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret) — this job is available as Jason.
  • Afternoon Delight (Rob Vicki’s Boss) — rob Delights and receive Vicki’s payout.

PowerPyx and GTABase say the other main Chapter 2 jobs must be cleared before Checking Out appears. KeenGamer separately notes that Billy’s two activities — Thrillbillies and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab — do not block the finale, but they can help if you need more money.

If a marker has not appeared, check the Story list and the character’s phone first. Do not assume one guide’s mission order is mandatory for every save.

2. How to reach $15,000 without unnecessary grinding

Start with Afternoon Delight

This is the clearest large payment. PowerPyx and GTABase report that after the robbery Vicki sends $10,000 through BuckMe on the next in-game day. KeenGamer says this covers most of the target; wait for the actual deposit before planning the remaining amount.

Use Moses if you are still short

  1. Complete the story mission Moses. It unlocks car sales at Watkins Auto Parts in Port Gellhorn.
  2. Open WAiNK and scan a vehicle. For Moses, look for the No Tracker status.
  3. Do not steal the car right outside Moses's garage: published guides warn that he may reject vehicles taken too close to the shop.
  4. Deliver the car carefully. Damage lowers its value, and the garage has a short cooldown after a sale.

The exact price depends on the vehicle and its condition. Do not assume every trip pays the same amount, and do not waste time on a tracked car — check WAiNK first.

Robberies and other payouts

Hit Avery Pawn and Hit Kingpin Bowling advance the Story list while giving you valuables to collect. After Slam Dunk, Dunk becomes a buyer for stolen goods; Chapter 2 fence rates are lower than those offered by some later buyers, so avoid selling everything automatically if you can wait for a better buyer in the next chapter.

Billy's activities can help top up your balance if you are still below the target. Impounded Vehicle is part of the story checklist, but do not treat it as a guaranteed cash source.

Protect your money. PowerPyx, GTABase, and The Games Wiki recommend depositing cash into BuckMe: money carried by the character can be lost after death or arrest. Watch the displayed balance and wait for Vicki's payment to arrive.

3. What to collect at Starlet Motel

The main reason not to rush Checking Out is that Starlet Motel stops being your home once the mission begins. Two card pickups in rooms that close are the most consistently flagged as potentially missable; there is one more card and two magazines worth collecting while you are there.

  • Vicki — Delights Dancers: on the floor beside the toilet in the motel office bathroom, past reception.
  • Victor Maravilla — Los Santos Corkers: on the refrigerator in the shared motel kitchen.
  • Bryson Powers: on top of a washing machine in the laundry room. The Click confirms the location but says guides are less consistent about whether this Rookie card remains accessible after Chapter 2. Pick it up now if you are still at the motel.
  • Help Me Help You Help Me: magazine at the reception desk.
  • Wizard Wang: magazine in the motel cafeteria.

Check Progress → Collections → Collectible Cards to make sure Vicki and Victor Maravilla are registered. The Games Wiki says 100% completion requires 15 of the 18 card sets, so missing these two individual cards should not by itself block the overall percentage. If you want the full collection, collect them before leaving anyway.

Do not confuse losing the motel with losing the region. You can return to Port Gellhorn after Checking Out; the warning concerns Starlet Motel and its interior rooms, not all of Kelly County.

4. When does Checking Out become available?

  1. Make sure BuckMe shows at least $15,000.
  2. Open Map → Story again and confirm the other main Chapter 2 jobs are complete.
  3. If the marker does not appear immediately, keep playing and pass some in-game time: KeenGamer reports that Cal's text arrives before the Move to Vice City objective.
  4. Make a manual save in a separate slot before the finale.
  5. Start Checking Out only after checking the Starlet Motel pickups.

The guides do not establish one fixed number of minutes before Cal's text arrives. We therefore do not promise an exact timer: check your phone and Story marker after meeting the requirements.

What is confirmed — and what remains uncertain

  • Agreed by PowerPyx and GTABase: Checking Out requires $15,000 and the other main Chapter 2 jobs to be complete; Afternoon Delight pays $10,000 through BuckMe on the next in-game day.
  • Supported by GTABase and car guides: Moses buys tracker-free cars after his story mission; WAiNK can identify the tracker status.
  • Supported by The Games Wiki and GTA 6 Collectibles Map: Checking Out closes Starlet Motel as a safehouse; collect Vicki and Victor Maravilla before the transition.
  • Unclear: whether the Bryson Powers Rookie pickup remains accessible after moving and the exact status of alternate card copies. Collect it early, but we do not label it definitively lost.
  • Editorial limitation: GTA6Pulse does not claim to have personally played this chapter. This guide synthesizes published walkthroughs; order and proceeds may vary by save and loot.
