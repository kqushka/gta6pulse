GTA 6 Pick Up a Gun: Get Your Weapons Back for Free

Updated November 23, 2026. Lucia's short Port Gellhorn errand helps rebuild your arsenal after Chapter 1. Check the green locker before spending money on a pistol.

Quick answer

  1. Check the Optional missions list on the map as you progress through early Chapter 2.
  2. Head to Gellhorn Outfitters, the Ammu-Nation on Port Gellhorn's western side near the beach.
  3. Inside, open the green weapon locker and choose Manage Weapons.
  4. Retrieve a weapon you own. This can complete the mission without a purchase.

Several guides place the mission after Girl's Trip, but published Chapter 2 lists differ slightly in the early sequence. If the marker is not available yet, continue the missions and check the Optional list.

Walkthrough

1. Find Gellhorn Outfitters

Open the map and select the Pick Up a Gun marker, or locate Gellhorn Outfitters on Port Gellhorn's western beach side. The objective sends Lucia to the local gun shop.

2. Check the green locker before buying

Inside Ammu-Nation, look for the green weapon locker. Interact with it and choose Manage Weapons. Published guides say it lets you retrieve guns you previously purchased or stored in the locker or in your personal vehicle's trunk.

This is the cheapest route: if a weapon you own is available, retrieve and equip it. Don't assume a temporary gun picked up from the ground during a firefight is part of your saved arsenal.

3. If the locker is empty, buy a weapon

Several current guides list the Scorpio Nipper .38 at $495 as the cheapest early Chapter 2 option. Prices or availability may vary, so confirm the amount shown at the shop before paying.

4. Leave the shop

After buying or retrieving a gun, leave Gellhorn Outfitters. Guides describe this as clearing the short mission; no separate cash reward is listed.

Why complete it?

  • Save your early Chapter 2 cash: check the locker first; retrieving an owned weapon may cost nothing.
  • Recover Chapter 1 purchases: you may not need to buy your guns again—check the locker.
  • For 100%: GTABase lists this mission as required for its 100% checklist, despite being optional to the story. Complete it if you're tracking full completion.
  • Don't confuse it with Gear Up: Gear Up is a separate Chapter 1 gun mission; Pick Up a Gun is Lucia's Port Gellhorn task in Chapter 2.

What the sources agree on

Several published walkthroughs agree on the location, the choice between buying and using the green locker, and that retrieving an owned weapon completes the objective.
