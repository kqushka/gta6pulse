GTA 6 Avery Pawn Shop: how to get the safe code

Updated November 24, 2026. During the Hit Avery Pawn robbery, the safe in the back office is locked behind a four-digit code. Do not copy a number from someone else's video: published playthroughs show different combinations, and you can obtain your own code from a shop employee.

Quick answer

  1. During the optional Hit Avery Pawn activity, aim your weapon at a clerk behind the counter and use the contextual threat action.
  2. Watch the prompt in the lower-right corner. After threatening staff, one employee should offer the Safe Code? interaction.
  3. Select it and remember the four-digit combination shown on your screen.
  4. Go behind the main counter through the staff-only door, enter the office at the back of the shop, and use the code on the safe beside the desk.

Important: do not kill or knock out every employee before getting the combination. VGC warns that once the staff are incapacitated, there is nobody left to ask.

Step-by-step walkthrough

1. Start Hit Avery Pawn

Avery Pawn is in Port Gellhorn. The activity is one of the optional Chapter 2 robberies. Enter the shop with your partner and begin the hold-up. PC Gamer also recommends wearing a mask; that is the writer's precaution, not a confirmed requirement for obtaining the code.

2. Get the combination from a clerk

Draw your weapon and aim it at the employees behind the counter. VGC lists Square for the Threaten action on PlayStation; on other platforms, follow the on-screen contextual prompt. Repeat the threat if Safe Code? has not appeared yet.

Check the employees one by one and watch the action prompt in the lower-right corner. PC Gamer received the code from the clerk behind the counter on the left, but that is one writer's observation, not a guarantee that the same employee will always be the one to ask.

3. Keep at least one employee conscious

Do not incapacitate all the staff before seeing and selecting Safe Code?. VGC says that if the employees are killed or knocked out before revealing the code, you will have nobody left to provide it. Deal with the safe first, then continue the robbery.

4. Find the safe in the back office

  1. Go behind the main counter.
  2. Find the door marked Staff Only and enter the back room.
  3. Go into the office on the right. The safe is beside the desk.
  4. Interact with the keypad, enter your four-digit code, and collect the contents.

VGC also notes that you can interact with the CCTV system and switch off the alarm in the office. These are additional actions in the room, not a way to discover the code.

Why codes from videos do not work

Published playthroughs show different combinations: PC Gamer got 5683, while VGC got 3156. That does not mean the route is inconsistent: both outlets note that the code differs between playthroughs. Enter only the number shown in your own game.

Do not waste attempts on someone else's digits. There is no confirmed universal Avery Pawn code shared by all players.

What is confirmed — and what is not

  • Confirmed by two independent walkthroughs: the code has four digits and is not the same for everyone; a clerk can reveal it during the robbery; the safe is in the office behind the counter.
  • Practical warning: do not incapacitate every employee before getting the code — VGC says there will be nobody left to ask.
  • Not established: that one specific clerk always gives the code, that every player sees the same interaction order, or that a fixed universal password exists.
  • Verification limit: GTA6Pulse has not personally played through this robbery. This route is based on PC Gamer and VGC guides published on November 21, 2026.
