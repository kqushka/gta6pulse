GTA 6 Avery Pawn Shop: how to get the safe code

Updated November 24, 2026. During the Hit Avery Pawn robbery, the back-office safe uses a four-digit code. Do not copy a number from someone else's video: published playthroughs show different combinations. The main method is to get your code from a clerk; another walkthrough reports a green note in the back room as a possible backup.

Quick answer

  1. During the optional Hit Avery Pawn activity, locate the safe in the office behind the counter. If Safe Code? does not appear, inspect the safe first and then return to the clerks: GamesRadar reports that the prompt may only appear after you have seen it.
  2. Aim your weapon at a clerk and use the contextual threat action. Watch the prompt in the lower-right corner.
  3. When Safe Code? appears, select it and remember the four-digit combination shown on your screen.
  4. If you could not get the code from a clerk, check the green note above the calendar beside the Office door. GTA BOOM reported finding it in its own run; this is a possible backup, not a guarantee for every save.
  5. Enter your code in the safe beside the desk.

Important: keep at least one employee conscious while trying to get the code from staff. VGC warns that once the staff are incapacitated, there may be nobody left to ask. Do not assume the note will always be available.

Step-by-step walkthrough

1. Start Hit Avery Pawn

Avery Pawn is in Port Gellhorn. The activity is one of the optional Chapter 2 robberies. Enter the shop with your partner and begin the hold-up. PC Gamer also recommends wearing a mask; that is the writer's precaution, not a confirmed requirement for obtaining the code.

2. Get the combination from a clerk

Draw your weapon and aim it at the employees behind the counter. VGC lists Square for the Threaten action on PlayStation; on other platforms, follow the on-screen contextual prompt. Check the employees one by one and watch the action prompt in the lower-right corner.

If Safe Code? does not appear after threatening staff, inspect the safe in the office first and return to the clerks: GamesRadar says the prompt may only appear after you have seen the safe. PC Gamer received the code from the clerk behind the counter on the left, but that is one writer's observation, not a guarantee that the same employee will always be the one to ask.

3. Keep at least one employee conscious

Keep at least one employee conscious while trying to get the code from staff. VGC says that if the employees are killed or knocked out before revealing it, you may have nobody left to ask. GTA BOOM separately reported a green note above the calendar beside the Office door as a backup in its own run, but its presence should not be treated as guaranteed in every save.

4. Find the safe in the back office

  1. Go behind the main counter.
  2. Find the door marked Staff Only and enter the back room.
  3. Go into the office on the right. The safe is beside the desk.
  4. Interact with the keypad, enter your four-digit code, and collect the contents.

If you did not get the code from a clerk, check the green note above the calendar beside the Office door. In GTA BOOM's November 22, 2026 walkthrough, it showed the same combination as the clerk. This is a useful place to check, not a confirmed universal method.

VGC also notes that you can interact with the CCTV system and switch off the alarm in the office. These are additional actions in the room, not a way to discover the code.

Why codes from videos do not work

Published playthroughs show different combinations: PC Gamer got 5683, VGC got 3156, and GTA BOOM got 4768. These are examples from separate runs, not alternate versions of one password. In GTA BOOM's run, the green note above the calendar also showed 4768. Enter only the number shown in your own game.

Do not waste attempts on someone else's digits. There is no confirmed universal Avery Pawn code; the backup note should not be assumed to appear in every save.

What is confirmed — and what is not

  • Supported by PC Gamer and VGC: the code has four digits and differs between playthroughs; a clerk can reveal it; the safe is in the office behind the counter.
  • Additional observations: GamesRadar says the Safe Code? prompt may appear only after seeing the safe; GTA BOOM reported a green note above the calendar in its November 22 run.
  • Not established: that the note appears in every save, that one specific clerk always gives the code, or that every player sees the same interaction order.
  • Verification limit: GTA6Pulse has not personally played through this robbery. This route is based on published PC Gamer, VGC, GamesRadar+ and GTA BOOM walkthroughs.
